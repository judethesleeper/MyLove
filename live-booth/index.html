Live Booth

This is the real two-phone version. One person creates a room, the other joins with the same link, and both phones take one shot each round. The finished strip combines both of you into one shared keepsake, then both of you download it to your phones.

  • 4 rounds total
  • Each round captures one photo from each phone
  • Both partners download the same final strip
  • Photos sync through the shared room during the session
Firebase is not configured yet. I already built the app structure, but before this can go live you need to paste your Firebase web config into `assets/js/firebase-config.js` and enable Firestore.
